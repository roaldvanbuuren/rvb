Handing over humanity. A scary, but real thought in today's world where the headlines are dominated by AI. Ruxandra Teslo, recently [wrote about this topic specifically](https://www.writingruxandrabio.com/p/handing-over-humanity).  In this fantastic post, she talks about her early experiences with leaders in AI labs, and the handing over of our society to AI, where a machine decides who gets paid what.

While I’m (most likely) with you that this is not an appealing thought, nor one that we should lay down and accept, I went down a train of thought to seek light at the end of the tunnel. Hear me out.‬

‪The last great age of progress was the Renaissance. It spawned an era of great beauty, ingenuity, and freedom of thought , and its aftermath produced things such as the printing press and electricity. ‬

‪However, most who created that innovation lived at the whims of a tyrant’s purse, virtue and continued interests. The Medici, Sforza, French Kings and popes.‬

‪The dawning age of AI may well give us a similar tyrant, this time a mechanical one, rather than biological. That sounds, and probably is, rather bleak.‬

‪For the light at the end of the tunnel, I’ll go back to the biological tyrants. The purse is what bought the freedom to think. The Medici allowed Galileo to stop teaching and start exploring. Same for Leonardo with Sforza, Ficino with Cosimo de Medici, and Michelangelo with Lorenzo de Medici.‬

‪If AI ends up paying a UBI, a far greater number of people may end up experiencing the freedom of exploring thoughts, and a whole deal of beauty and innovation could come out of that.‬

‪As I said, trying to find the light at the end of a dark tunnel.
