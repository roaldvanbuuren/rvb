In gaming, we often see AI as some evil tool used by the overlords to outsource work. But where AI is actually strong is in giving every person access to technology to build quicker and faster, and more than anything, better and more accessible. So rather than empowering large corporations, it’s actually a situation where David can’t just compete with Goliath, David can beat it.

Now I get it. AI is often called out as the reason for layoffs around game studios in recent months. However, I think it’s a very convenient scapegoat for overhiring and mismanagement before, but mostly during and after COVID, and for overestimating projected revenues. AI is just a tool like any other technology in the past, and whether it’ll be used for good or for bad is determined by the user.

So I believe that in the gaming ecosystem, those who believe in grassroots and in the community should use it rather than fight it. Be David against Goliath, and build the games that we want, that the community and the gamers want, rather than what makes the most money for the large corporations.
