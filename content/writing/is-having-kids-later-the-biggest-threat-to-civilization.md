Is people getting kids at an older age (or not at all) the biggest threat to civilization?

I’m not talking about a greying population, where there aren’t enough young, working people to pay pensions for older generations. That’s a real issue but not one I’m talking about right now.

Maybe I’m slightly biased here as a father and someone who always wanted to be father but I believe children give you the biggest purpose in life. Not just to be a good parent, but to create a better place for the next generation.

When children are born later people spend large portions of their life thinking in The now. This is normal behaviour for people in young adulthood but as we get older we should be thinking about what the world looks like after us and what legacy will leave behind. When is happens later and later in life, the now becomes important longer and longer. This has an impact on things such as climate change, technology, politics, democracy, and building things that are impactful rather than nice to have.

Because most of these things to create a better future require pain and sacrifice that make them now more difficult but the future better.

When the now is more important than the future that sacrifice is not worth it. And civilizations probably need people who are willing to sacrifice something today for people they will never meet, now more than ever.
