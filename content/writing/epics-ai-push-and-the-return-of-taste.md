You’d be living in a cave if you missed it. Tim Sweeney, CEO of Epic, announced that they’re “retooling for maximum AI acceleration”. As you can expect with anything around AI, the response was extreme and divided. But I think there is a deeper underlying theme around Epic that is interesting to explore.

Going back in time, Unreal Engine was one of the first engines to making games more accessible. Over time, the engines became so good, that the technical element of making games became less important. Gaming shifted from being a mostly technical industry to a creative one; with engines doing a lot of the heavy lifting, the design, story, look and feel became more important.

Back to today; AI drives this even further. Not only does the technical side get even cheaper and easier, so does asset generation. Whats now left is “taste”: the vision, the world, the story.

One final thought, is that what Tim said might be true; Epic can build more stuff, better. But is that what we need? Steam already has too many games to keep up with, and that is not even including what’s happening on Roblox. We need more quality, deeper narratives, more replayability, a transmedia approach, and MORE COMMUNITY. If AI can help offer that, I’m all for it.
