What many founders forget is that most of their customers see the world as it is today, not how it will look in 6-24 months. So their feedback only speaks to now.

Your job as a founder is to hold both. Build for today, but figure out how the world looks in 12-18 months and how your customer will be living in it.

The reason I know is pretty simple: I spend a meaningful amount of my time researching this by looking at other industries, watching how behavior is shifting, understanding what’s coming. That’s my job.

Netflix is a good example, as nobody was asking for streaming in the early 2000s, people were fine renting DVDs. Hastings could see where the world was going and built for that world before his customers could even imagine it. By the time they understood they wanted it, Netflix was already there.

Customer feedback is one input, rather than the answer.
