I’ve never been a Marvel fan. I watched some of the movies and series over the years but it never truly spoke to me.

As a former vivid Overwatch fan (pre-franchising mind you ;), when Marvel Rivals came out my ears perked. Then seeing it every where around me in my universe; esports teams picking up players, lots of big streamers playing it and interesting tournaments happening, I could no longer ignore it.

I’ve spent a few evenings in a row playing it now, watching streamers and following tournaments. Initially it made me want to get my McCree on, but now I am and loving the game (Bucky ftw). Not just for the gameplay, but I am getting drawn into the universe, trying to understand the lore and community memes. I just told my wife (to her delight), that I want to watch the movies and series — we started with Captain America last night.

Marvel Rivals and its approach just shows the power of transmedia. If you engage the user in a way that speaks to them, you can draw them in and make them a life long fan. This is how you make a game stick around in this overcrowded space.
