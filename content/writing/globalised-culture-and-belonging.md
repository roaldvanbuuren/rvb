A lot of people don’t seem to see the issue in the globalization of culture. And at first sight, it might seem a side product of simple evolution, something innocent, or even good to change away from problematic pasts of nations.

But there is a dangerous underlying issue that is feeding into a lot of the problems surfacing now, especially with young people. That is a lack of belonging and sense of identity. While countries and their cultures were far from perfect, they did give us something to feel a sense of pride in, something to belong to. Take that away and people will go looking for it somewhere else. For a lot of lonely young people, that’s whatever corner of the internet welcomes them in first, and some of those corners are pretty dark.

I think the answer is probably in communities people choose for themselves. Clubs, games, fandoms, local groups. Places where you’re known, and where you matter to someone.
