I actually wanted to start this post differently, but my struggle to do what I wanted highlights the actual problem I wanted to post about: I cannot find back the research paper that was published last week on how Europe should deal with technology, the one that was doing its rounds here on X. The issue I ran into; there are so many papers released each week, that it’s impossible to find any back.

As I said, this highlights my point; while all these papers might have different nuances, they all have the same goal, so the time of writing one with a slightly different path to achieve the goal is over. The political blueprint is there.

As European citizens, our time of writing papers is over. We now need to stop moaning, action what we can to ensure our politicians start sailing the map, and do what we can ourselves. No more papers, more building.

Yes, going to the notary is a pain. But you have to, so get on with it, start a business, and get going. Yes, funding is hard to get. Does that mean you shouldn’t even try and just go on X to complain? Heck no. Try and try again. Grants are confusing? Spend some time on it and I’m sure you’ll figure it out. Excuses will always be there no matter what.

Maybe that’s what we’re good at in Europe really, finding excuses to procrascinate. Stop moaning, start doing.
